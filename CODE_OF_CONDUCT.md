# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful and productive experience for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while still asking precise questions.
- Keep feedback focused on the work.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality.
- Avoid sharing sensitive operational or subscriber data.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sharing credentials, secrets, or exploit details in public channels.
- Disruptive or bad-faith participation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, or pull requests that violate this code of conduct. Repeated or severe violations may lead to temporary or permanent restrictions from project participation.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to the project maintainer through a private GitHub contact channel when possible.
